Hi! I'm Mol, or Shawn as the banner says. This site is always changing and it's a place where I talk about my normal thoughts and hobbies.

This is the Mole hill, home to all of my ideas and other things. Feel free to poke around and see what's up. I'm still working on more rooms and maybe eventually somewhere you guys can talk.

For now, I have a place for all the games I've played, Some information on a dnd campaign I'm running, and a journal where I post my surface level thoughts!

Eventually I'll have this place more decorated with art and stories and other cool little things I create, but for now it's pretty empty. Some of these pages are mobile friendly but most aren't, so view on mobile at your own risk!
