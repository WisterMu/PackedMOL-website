Day 4

I met a cult of ghouls...or a bunch of religious ones? I never really understood the difference.

Today was kind of interesting. I think I might be getting used to this Mojave lifestyle. I woke up a bit late today, and I guess Victor got impatient because he was gone when I went outside. I did get to meet the owner of the building, Jeannie. I didn't realize anyone actually owned the place but she didn't seem too bothered with me mooching off Victor's room.

I asked around a bit to see if anyone knew of the man in a checkered suit, and was directed to Manny, one of the guards of Novac. He was holed up in the mouth of the giant creature in town, which I found an interesting spot. Good enough lookout I suppose.

He did have info on the guy who shot me, but he wanted a favor first. Some ghouls took over the nearby Repconn facility, where people from Novac get their junk. He didn't mention where they came from, but it seemed like an easy enough job. I decided to accept his trade, and started walking over to the facility.
Immediately upon approaching, I was attacked by some ghouls. I noticed several naked ones, but also some that had some sort of uniform?

I quickly got a rundown of the situation upon entering the building. I met these two fellas, who informed me of some "demons" that invaded their basement. Chris was also oddly insistent that he was a ghoul, although he was the most lively ghoul that I'd ever seen. Jason was just running a cult I think.

He seemed to care for his people though, so I decided to check out the "demons" downstairs. While searching for them, I came across a room full of traps, and a ghoul ready to shoot me. Luckily he wasn't feeling too trigger happy. He introduced himself as Harland, and asked if I could find his friend while I cleared the basement, which I agreed to. Mainly because I'm already down here.

I also found one of the "demons" that the ghouls were talking about, and he was willing to chat too! It's so weird how many people are willing to chat with me. Why couldn't the guy who shot me be this nice.

Him and his skull friend, "Antler", came here to find a shipment of stealth boys, and he agreed to leave once he found out where they were. I ended up coming across Harland's friend while looking for those stealth boys, or what happened to her at least.

I updated Harland on his friend and told Davison about the shipment being returned. I was slightly worried that this would break out into a fight, since he didn't seem to believe it. Luckily Antler was there to talk him down, and he left peacefully. Antler seems like a chill person. Too bad I can't hear them at all.

I returned to the group of ghouls upstairs and finally updated them on what happened. I guess they need me for something else, because they asked me to stay and meet them in the basement later. I've decided to take a quick cat nap for now, because this whole running around business has me sweating.

Chat ya later,

Jackie
